StayFree Local Sync

Pairing and cross-device stats go straight to the StayFree app on your phone over your Wi-Fi. Nothing is sent to StayFree or SensorTower; their analytics and data-collection endpoints are blocked.

How to pair

  1. Install the patched StayFree app (with the Local device sync and In-app QR scanner patches) and open it once.
  2. In the extension dashboard press Pair New Device → StayFree Android App. The extension finds the phone on your Wi-Fi by itself and shows a QR code.
  3. On the phone, go to Settings → Paired Devices → Pair New Device → Browser Extension: StayFree's own scanner opens. Point it at the QR code. (You can also long-press the StayFree icon → Scan QR code, or type the code instead.)
  4. If the phone isn't found, enter its address above. The scanner screen shows it under This phone's local sync address (port 8787 is added automatically). A DHCP reservation on your router keeps it from changing.

The phone and this computer must be on the same network, and the StayFree app must be running on the phone (it normally stays running in the background).
